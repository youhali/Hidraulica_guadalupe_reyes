# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Three audiences with equal weight, all in Mexican industry, reading in Spanish, many on a phone:

- Plant and workshop maintenance people with a stopped machine who need a repair or quote quickly.
- Buyers who already know which hydraulic part they need.
- Companies that want a hydraulic power unit built to order.

## Product Purpose

Website of Hidráulica Francisco, a hydraulic sales and technical service business in Ecatepec de Morelos, run by Jose Francisco Reyes Flores, hydraulic engineer. It must show the real work and turn visits into WhatsApp conversations. Success is a visitor who sends a quote request.

## Positioning

A hands-on shop that repairs, maintains, diagnoses and builds hydraulic equipment itself, shown through its own workshop photographs, with an identity rooted in Mexica mastery of water: "Potencia y precisión hidráulica con raíz de piedra."

## Operating Context

Static site deployed on Render from the GitHub repository. Quote requests go through WhatsApp, with no backend. Visitors often arrive by scanning the QR on the business card.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services confirmed by the existing site: hydraulic repair (pumps, valves, cylinders, complete systems), preventive and corrective maintenance, fabrication of custom hydraulic units, diagnosis (pressure, flow, leaks), sale of spare parts.
- Multiple pages, chosen by the owner's family. Plain static HTML, CSS and JS with no build step.
- The quote form must keep opening WhatsApp with the assembled message.
- Undecided or unknown: years in business, number of clients, certifications, warranty terms, prices, email address. Do not invent them.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Hidráulica Francisco. The repository and Render URL say "guadalupe-reyes"; that is not the public name.
- Logo: `img/Logo.png`, the jaguar emblem with water, pipes and step-fret border.
- Prehispanic (Mexica) theme is required and must be kept, with more original designs than the previous version.
- Language: Spanish, addressing the visitor as "tú".

## Product Principles

- Show the real work before making any claim.
- Every page leaves the visitor one tap from WhatsApp.
- Serve repair, parts and fabrication visitors equally; none should have to hunt for their path.
- The heritage carries real meaning about water engineering; it is never costume.
- Fast on a mid-range phone on mobile data, readable in a workshop.
